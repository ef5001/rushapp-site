Safety tools in the app

If someone makes you uncomfortable or breaks our rules, you don't need to wait on us — you can act right away from inside the app:

  • Block a user from their profile or from a chat thread to stop all contact with them.
  • Report a user from their profile or from a chat thread. Reports are reviewed by our team, and we may remove content or suspend accounts that violate our Terms of Service.
If you or someone else is in immediate danger, please contact local emergency services first. Use in-app reporting and email us afterward so we can follow up.

Manage your account

Account controls live in the app under Settings:

  • Export My Data — download a copy of the personal information associated with your account.
  • Delete Account — permanently remove your profile, photos, albums, messages, and associated data from our systems.

Trouble with either of these, or a question we haven't answered here? Email support@rushapp.io and we'll help.
