Privacy Policy

Rush — a location-based social app for adults. Last updated: November 5, 2026.

Rush is intended only for people 18 years of age or older. This policy explains what we collect, why, how it is shared, and the choices and rights you have. Please also read our Terms of Service.

Rush ("Rush", "we", "us", "our") is operated by Chaotic Neutral LLC ("the Operator"), which is the data controller responsible for your personal information. By creating an account or using Rush, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. You can reach us about privacy at support@rushapp.io.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the service. Everything below is provided by you or generated as you use the app.

Account information

  • Your email address and a password (used to sign in; passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form).
  • Your date of birth, used to confirm you are 18 or older.
  • A display name.
  • A record of the consents you give (for example, your age confirmation and your consent to processing sensitive information), including when they were given.

Profile information you choose to provide

  • Photos for your profile, and any photos or videos you add to private albums.
  • Optional details such as bio, height, weight, body type, and "looking for" preferences.
  • Sensitive information you optionally choose to add — including data that may reveal your race or ethnicity, sexual orientation, and health information such as HIV status. See Section 4.

Location

  • Your approximate device location, used to show you people nearby and to display distance.
  • Location is collected only while you are using the app (foreground), with one exception: during a safety mode session that you start, Rush also uses your location in the background (with your "Always" permission) and shares it with the trusted contact you chose. It stops when the session ends.
  • When you check in somewhere, the place you chose is shown with your profile for up to two hours. When you host a plan, its place (or, if you don't add one, your approximate location) is shown to people who can see the plan.
  • Your position is shown to other users only as an approximate, fuzzed location on the map and as a rounded distance — never your exact coordinates.

Content and activity

  • Messages and media you send in one-to-one chats, group chats and plan chats, including message requests.
  • Posts and check-ins (a place, a caption, photos or a short video), and stories, which disappear after 24 hours. You can see who viewed your story.
  • Plans you host or ask to join, including the note you add to a join request.
  • Vouches you write or receive.
  • Interactions such as likes, matches, blocks, reports, and your online / last-active status.

Safety mode

  • If you use safety mode, the name and phone number of the trusted contact you pick from your phone's contacts. Rush saves only the contacts you pick; it does not upload your address book.
  • While a session runs: who you're meeting, the place, your check-in time and your location. Anyone with the session's link (the person you sent it to) can see these until the session ends. Rush does not send texts; you send the link yourself from your own phone.

Camera and microphone

  • Used only when you take a photo or record a video in the app. Nothing is recorded otherwise.

Device information

  • A push-notification token for the device, so we can deliver notifications you have enabled.
Ads without tracking. Rush shows banner ads from Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads only: Rush does not read your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), does not ask to track you, includes no third-party analytics trackers, and does not track you across other apps or websites. To serve and measure an ad, Google receives limited technical information such as your IP address and device type.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and operate your account and profile.
  • To show you nearby people and power matching, likes, and messaging.
  • To send notifications you have turned on.
  • To keep the community safe — reviewing reports, enforcing our Terms, and preventing abuse.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app.

4. Sensitive information

Some profile fields are optional and may reveal sensitive information about you, such as race or ethnicity, sexual orientation, or health information like HIV status. Providing these is entirely your choice — you can leave them blank, and you can edit or remove them at any time in the app.

Because this is "special category" data under the GDPR, we process it only on the basis of your explicit consent, which you give when you create your account and which you confirm by choosing to add these details to your profile. When you add sensitive information, you understand it will be shown to other users as part of your profile. We use it only to display your profile and power the filters and features you ask for. We never sell it, and never use it for advertising or profiling. You can withdraw your consent at any time by removing the information from your profile or deleting your account.

5. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of the following rights over your personal data. For users in the EEA and UK these are guaranteed by the GDPR; we extend the same controls to all users wherever we can.

  • Access — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you. You can export your data in the app (Settings → Export My Data), or request it at support@rushapp.io.
  • Rectification — correct or update your information by editing your profile in the app.
  • Erasure — delete your account from Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, photos, albums, messages, and associated data from our systems.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (the in-app export is provided as JSON).
  • Restriction and objection — ask us to limit or stop certain processing, including processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — where we rely on your consent (including for sensitive information and notifications), withdraw it at any time.
  • Control notifications and visibility through the app's Settings and Safety & Privacy screens.
  • Block or report other users.

To exercise any right, use the in-app controls above or contact support@rushapp.io. We do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you using solely automated processing.

If you are in the EEA or UK and believe we have not handled your data properly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to access and delete it, to correct it, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Because we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, there is nothing to opt out of in that respect. We limit the use of sensitive personal information to providing the service, as described above. The categories of personal information we collect are described in Section 1: identifiers, account and contact data, approximate location, user content (photos, messages), sensitive information you choose to provide, and product-interaction data.

6. How your information is shared

With other users

Your profile — including your display name, photos, the profile details you fill in, and your approximate distance — is visible to other users of Rush. Messages you send are delivered to the people you send them to. In a group or plan chat, the other members see your name, photo, and what you send there. A plan's host sees your join request and its note. Posts, check-ins and stories are visible to other users nearby, and vouches appear on a profile only after its owner approves them. Please share only what you are comfortable making visible to other members.

Profile photos are served from a public content-delivery URL so they load quickly for other users; anyone with the direct link to a photo can view it. Private album media is access-controlled.

We may disclose information if required by law, to respond to legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users, the public, or the Operator.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not use your sensitive information (including health or sexual-orientation data) for advertising or profiling.

7. Data retention

Some content is removed automatically: stories after 24 hours, a check-in's "here now" status after two hours, a plan's chat 48 hours after the plan ends, and ignored message requests after 90 days. When you report someone, a copy of the reported messages is kept with the report so our team can review it.

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data and stored media. We may retain limited records where required for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention purposes, and only for as long as necessary for those purposes.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, and access to profile data is restricted by row-level security rules on our backend. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Children

Rush is for adults only. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will remove it.

10. International users and data transfers

Rush is operated from, and data is stored in facilities located in, the United States. If you use Rush from outside the United States — including from the EEA or UK — your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States. Where required, such transfers are made on the basis of appropriate safeguards or your consent. By using Rush from outside the United States, you understand your information will be processed in the United States.
