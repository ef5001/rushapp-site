Privacy Policy

Rush — a location-based social app for adults. Last updated: August 2, 2026.

Rush is intended only for people 18 years of age or older. This policy explains what we collect, why, how it is shared, and the choices and rights you have. Please also read our Terms of Service.

This app ("Rush", "we", "us") is operated by [OPERATOR NAME] ("the Operator"). By creating an account or using Rush, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the service. Everything below is provided by you or generated as you use the app.

Account information

  • Your email address and a password (used to sign in; passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form).
  • Your date of birth, used to confirm you are 18 or older.
  • A display name.

Profile information you choose to provide

  • Photos for your profile, and any photos or videos you add to private albums.
  • Optional details such as bio, height, weight, body type, and "looking for" preferences.
  • Sensitive information you optionally choose to add — including data that may reveal your race or ethnicity, sexual orientation, and health information such as HIV status. See Section 4.

Location

  • Your approximate device location, used to show you people nearby and to display distance.
  • Location is collected only while you are using the app (foreground). We do not track your location in the background.
  • Your position is shown to other users only as an approximate, fuzzed location on the map and as a rounded distance — never your exact coordinates.

Content and activity

  • Messages and media you send in chats.
  • Interactions such as likes, matches, blocks, reports, and your online / last-active status.

Device information

  • A push-notification token for the device, so we can deliver notifications you have enabled.
We do not use tracking or advertising SDKs. Rush does not read your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), does not include third-party ad or analytics trackers, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and operate your account and profile.
  • To show you nearby people and power matching, likes, and messaging.
  • To send notifications you have turned on.
  • To keep the community safe — reviewing reports, enforcing our Terms, and preventing abuse.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app.

3. How your information is shared

With other users

Your profile — including your display name, photos, the profile details you fill in, and your approximate distance — is visible to other users of Rush. Messages you send are delivered to the people you send them to. Please share only what you are comfortable making visible to other members.

Profile photos are served from a public content-delivery URL so they load quickly for other users; anyone with the direct link to a photo can view it. Private album media is access-controlled.

For legal and safety reasons

We may disclose information if required by law, to respond to legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users, the public, or the Operator.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not use your sensitive information (including health or sexual-orientation data) for advertising or profiling.

4. Sensitive information

Some profile fields are optional and may reveal sensitive information about you, such as race or ethnicity, sexual orientation, or health information like HIV status. Providing these is entirely your choice — you can leave them blank, and you can edit or remove them at any time in the app.

When you add sensitive information to your profile, you understand it will be shown to other users as part of your profile. We use this information only to display your profile and power the filters and features you ask for. We never sell it and never use it for advertising.

5. Your rights and choices

You can, at any time:

  • Access and update your information by editing your profile in the app.
  • Delete your account from Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, photos, albums, messages, and associated data from our systems.
  • Control notifications and visibility through the app's Settings and Safety & Privacy screens.
  • Block or report other users.

California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to access and delete it, to correct it, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Because we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, there is nothing to opt out of in that respect. We limit the use of sensitive personal information to providing the service, as described above.

The categories of personal information we collect are described in Section 1: identifiers, account and contact data, approximate location, user content (photos, messages), sensitive information you choose to provide, and product-interaction data. To exercise any right, contact us at support@rushapp.io. You may also delete your account directly in the app.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data and stored media. We may retain limited records where required for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention purposes, for no longer than necessary.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, and access to profile data is restricted by row-level security rules on our backend. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Children

Rush is for adults only. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will remove it.

9. International users

Rush is operated from, and data is stored in facilities located in, the United States. If you use Rush from outside the United States, you consent to processing your information in the United States.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app.
